# Porter-Delivery Analysis
The porter analysis exploring delivery operations data to gain insights into business performance, order trends, and operational efficiency. It aims to support better decision-making through data-driven insights, helping identify opportunities to improve delivery performance, optimize resources, and enhance overall service quality.


**Objective:**
The objective of Porter Analysis is to identifying order trends, market performance, and delivery operations by analyzing delivery data. This analyis focuses on order volume distribution across market, peak order hour, and the busiest day of the week to understand demand pattern. Using Microsoft Excel for analyzing delivery data, the project aims to to transform this data into meaningful business insights. 

**Market Performance** :  Understand in which market the delivery of data has been raised across different markets.

**Order Trend :** Peak Ordering Hous and order Volume by day of week.

****Delivery Performance**: Analyze Average delivery time, and percentage of orders delivered within 30 minutes.

****Store Categories Analysis**: Order Volume and delivery time across different markets and different stores categories.
